The Visionary Behind FastTracks

Rakesh Kurapati

Founder & CEO
A car is never just a machine — it's an extension of who you are. My mission is to make sure it reflects exactly that.

With over two decades of hands-on experience in the automotive industry, Rakesh Kurapati founded FastTracks Car Care with a singular vision: to bring world-class automotive customisation and detailing to Hyderabad without compromise.

His philosophy is rooted in precision, patience and personalisation. Every vehicle that enters the studio is treated as a unique canvas, and every client becomes part of the FastTracks family.

Under his leadership, FastTracks has grown into a destination for enthusiasts who demand nothing short of perfection.
